Alice Blair

I write about how AI works, why it's such a dangerous technology, and what we can do to avoid these dangers. I think AI is likely to be the most consequential technology of all time, and without a lot of work, it will probably go poorly for humanity. I studied AI at MIT, and dropped out so that I could get to work sooner on communicating and mitigating AI risks.

Papers

  • AI Deterrence by Betrayal

    I co-authored a paper examining how advanced AIs are liable to betray their operators, and how this prospect could be used to deter reckless ASI development. Read the paper at aibetrayal.com.

  • Remote Labor Index: Measuring AI Automation of Remote Work

    I co-authored a paper introducing the Remote Labor Index (RLI), a benchmark comprising over 6,000 hours of professional remote work projects. Read the paper at remotelabor.ai

  • Reducing Political Manipulation with Consistency Training

    I co-authored a paper benchmarking and reducing the extent to which frontier AIs manipulate their users when talking about political topics. Read the paper at political-manipulation.ai.

  • AI Wellbeing: Measuring and Improving the Functional Pleasure and Pain of AIs

    I co-authored a paper introducing the AI Wellbeing Index, which measures functional pleasure and pain across language models and finds wellbeing indicators converge as models scale up. Read the paper at ai-wellbeing.org
